SERVICES SHOWCASE

One Exam. Multiple Career Pathways.

Explore the major administrative service branches that you can enter through the UPSC CSE.

Indian Administrative Service (IAS)

Administration & Governance

The IAS is the premier administrative civil service of India. IAS officers manage local governance, direct central developmental projects, and draft policies.

Core Areas of Responsibility

  • Managing local administration and development policies in districts.
  • Formulating policies and advising Ministers at State and Central levels.
  • Implementing and monitoring government developmental programmes.
  • Managing crises and responding to natural or man-made disasters.
Governance Leader

Indian Police Service (IPS)

Security, Law & Order

The IPS commands law enforcement, criminal investigation, intelligence structures, and internal security across India.

Core Areas of Responsibility

  • Maintaining law and order and preventing crime in districts and cities.
  • Leading intelligence agencies, security commands, and counter-terrorism squads.
  • Managing public safety, crowd control, and border security administration.
  • Reforming police organizations and implementing modern law enforcement tech.
Law Enforcement

Indian Foreign Service (IFS)

Diplomacy & Global Relations

IFS officers serve in Embassies, High Commissions, and the Ministry of External Affairs, managing global treaties and diplomacy.

Core Areas of Responsibility

  • Representing India in Embassies, High Commissions, and Consulates abroad.
  • Negotiating bilateral and multilateral treaties on trade, defense, and climate.
  • Protecting the interests of Indian citizens residing outside India.
  • Promoting India's cultural and economic interests globally.
Global Diplomacy

Indian Revenue Service (IRS)

Financial Administration & Enforcement

IRS officers protect national treasury boundaries, collect taxes, reform fiscal frameworks, and investigate economic offenses.

Core Areas of Responsibility

  • Administering Income Tax, Customs, and Goods & Services Tax (GST).
  • Investigating money laundering, tax evasion, and economic offenses.
  • Designing fiscal policy and revenue collection systems.
  • Facilitating trade compliance and border revenue enforcement.
Economic Security

Other Central Services (Group A)

Specialized Administration

Recruits for key administrative portfolios including audit services, corporate law enforcement, and defense estate administration.

Key Service Branches Include

  • Indian Audit and Accounts Service (IA&AS): Financial auditing.
  • Indian Postal/Railway Services: National logistics & connectivity.
  • Indian Defense Accounts/Estates Services: Defense estate administration.
  • Indian Corporate Law Service (ICLS): Corporate governance.
Public Infrastructure
